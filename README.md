# Conservation-Genomics

This repository provides a streamlined pipeline and the code used for our manuscript **_Shared landscape, divergent genomes: Comparative population genomics of co-occurring flora in an isolated Australian mountain range_** (https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.09.765275). We used genomic data from nine co-existing plant species with contrasting life-histories and ecological traits across Gariwerd, a threatened biodiversity hotspot in south-eastern Australia. We found that overall genetic structure was weak across species, though spatial patterns were broadly consistent, with higher genomic divergence in central zones and higher diversity in the south. However, species differed markedly in how their genetic variation correlated with geographic and environmental distances. Overall, our results show that co-occurring species can share broad spatial patterns of genomic variation while responding differently to the same landscape.
